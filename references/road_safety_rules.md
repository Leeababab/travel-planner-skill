# Road Safety & Self-Driving Regulations Reference

## 🇳🇿 New Zealand Self-Driving Code

### 1. Left-Hand Drive & Roundabouts
- Drive on the left side of the road at all times.
- Give way to all traffic approaching from your right at roundabouts (traffic circles).
- When turning right across oncoming traffic, give way to oncoming vehicles turning left.

### 2. Single-Lane Bridges (單線橋判讀)
- **Red Circle with Large Red Arrow**: You DO NOT have right of way. You MUST stop at the white line and yield to oncoming vehicles.
- **Blue Square with Large White Arrow**: You HAVE priority, but always slow down to confirm the bridge is clear before crossing.

### 3. Mountain Passes & Engine Braking (引擎制動)
- Crown Range (1,121m), Arthur's Pass (SH73), Lindis Pass (SH8), Milford Road (SH94).
- On steep descent grades, shift your automatic transmission to Manual Mode (M1/M2/L) to use engine braking.
- Avoid riding the foot brakes continuously to prevent brake fluid boiling and brake fade.

### 4. Slow Vehicle Passing Bays
- If vehicles accumulate behind you on narrow two-lane highways, pull into designated "Slow Vehicle Bays" on the left and allow trailing traffic to pass safely.

## 🇯🇵 Japan Self-Driving & Transit Code

### 1. Road Priority & Right Turns
- Drive on the left side.
- Straight-ahead and left-turning traffic have absolute right of way over right-turning traffic.
- Pedestrians at crosswalks have absolute priority.

### 2. Expressway ETC System
- Rent an ETC (Electronic Toll Collection) card with your rental vehicle for seamless highway toll gate passage at 20 km/h.
- Settle tolls upon vehicle return.

### 3. Parking Regulations
- Never park on narrow streets. Always use Coin Parking (コインパーキング) or hotel designated lots.
- Lock flaps (フラップ式) automatically raise underneath the car; pay at the centralized terminal before driving away.
