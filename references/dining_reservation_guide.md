# Dining Reservations & Platform Guide

## 🇯🇵 Japan Platform Ecosystem

| Platform | Best For | Typical Lead Time | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **食べログ (Tabelog)** | Store details, reviews, ranking, direct online booking | 14 - 30 days | Rating 3.5+ = Top 3% in Japan; 3.8+ = Exceptional. |
| **TableCheck** | Yakiniku, Sukiyaki, Teppanyaki, high-end hotel dining | 30 - 60 days | Real-time seat inventory; requires credit card guarantee. |
| **OMAKASE.in** | Michelin sushi, yakitori, kaiseki | 30 - 60 days (often 1st of month) | Strict cancellation policies; requires SMS registration. |
| **一休.com (Ikyu)** | Luxury hotel afternoon tea, scenic fine dining | 14 - 45 days | Best for curated course discounts and window seats. |

## 🇳🇿 New Zealand Dining & Winery Bookings

- High-demand restaurants in Queenstown (Fergburger Click & Collect, Flame Bar & Grill, Botswana Butchery) and Mount Cook Village (The Hermitage Panorama Buffet) require 1-4 weeks advance booking during peak summer (Nov - Feb).
- Winery lunches in Central Otago / Wanaka (Rippon, Amisfield, Mt Difficulty) should be booked 2-6 weeks ahead.
