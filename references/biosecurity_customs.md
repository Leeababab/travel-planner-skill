# Biosecurity, Quarantine & Customs Declaration Reference

## 🇳🇿 New Zealand Biosecurity (Ministry for Primary Industries - MPI)

### 1. Mandatory Outdoor Gear Declaration
- **Hiking Boots / Camping Gear**: You MUST declare all used outdoor footwear, tents, and hiking poles on the Passenger Arrival Card (Tick **YES**).
- Wash and scrub all dirt, soil, and organic matter from hiking soles before departure. Clean boots pass the inspection lane in under 2 minutes.

### 2. Strictly Prohibited Items
- Fresh fruit, fresh vegetables, fresh meat, poultry, honey, bee products, seeds, bulbs, and unpasteurized dairy.
- Failure to declare prohibited goods results in an instant, non-negotiable **NZD $400 fine**.

## 🇯🇵 Japan Customs & Visit Japan Web

### 1. Digital Registration
- Register flight details, passport, and customs declarations on [Visit Japan Web](https://vjw-lp.digital.go.jp/en/) 1-2 weeks prior to departure.
- Save the Immigration QR Code and Customs Declaration QR Code as screenshots on your mobile device.

### 2. Prohibited Animal & Plant Products
- Meat products (beef jerky, sausages, pork bento, dried meat) are strictly prohibited without official inspection certificates.
- Fresh tropical fruits (mangoes, apples) are banned.
